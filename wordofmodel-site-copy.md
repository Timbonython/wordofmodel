## Copy rules for anything written under this brand

Keep these on file. They're what stops the site drifting into the same language as every competitor.

- **Never write "AI visibility".** That's their category name and it's a commodity. Ours is what's being said.
- **No em dashes.** Spaced hyphen or a full stop.
- **No buzzwords.** No leverage, no unlock, no game-changer, no empower, no solution. If a sentence would survive on any SaaS site in the world, rewrite it.
- **Short sentences do the work.** Vary the length hard. The three-word ones land.
- **Concrete over adjective.** "Seven companies were named and you weren't one" beats "poor visibility performance" every time.
- **Never overclaim the result.** The honesty is the product. A scan that always says you're invisible gets found out in a week.
- **Say the hard thing plainly and then say what to do about it.** Never one without the other.
